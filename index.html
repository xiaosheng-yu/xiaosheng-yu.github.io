👨‍🏫 Biography

Xiaosheng Yu (于晓升) is an Associate Professor at the Faculty of Robot Science and Engineering, Northeastern University, China. His research interests include computer vision, image processing, machine learning, medical image analysis, visual saliency detection, and novel view synthesis.

He has long been engaged in algorithmic research and application-oriented system development in computer vision and intelligent perception, with representative work covering retinal fundus image analysis, brain tumor segmentation, RGB-T salient object detection, and neural rendering.

🔍 Research Interests

Computer Vision: visual saliency detection, RGB-T perception, object detection and segmentation. Medical Image Analysis: optic disc/cup detection and segmentation, brain tumor segmentation, vessel segmentation. Machine Learning: multimodal fusion, contrastive learning, representation learning, deep neural networks. Novel View Synthesis: NeRF-based rendering, feature radiance fields, frequency-enhanced neural rendering.
